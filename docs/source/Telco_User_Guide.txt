Excel ingestion workflow
1.   Download the governed Excel template for the selected evidence domain.
2.   Populate required columns and preserve the Product ID or approved source key.
3.   Upload .xlsx; the platform performs malware scanning, file-size/type checks and tenant-safe quarantine.
4.   Preview the workbook and select the worksheet and header row.
5.   Map columns to the canonical product schema; reuse saved mappings only when version-compatible.
6.   Validate required fields, data types, references, duplicates, effective dates and commercial-product-to-service-to-resource relationships.
7.   Resolve errors before publishing; warnings can proceed only under configured policy.
8.   Reconcile the Excel content with live-system records using source precedence, freshness and steward-approved conflict handling.

3. Telco Product-to-Service-to-Resource Evidence
Commercial rationalisation cannot rely on CRM and CPQ alone. The agent must understand how each commercial product decomposes into
customer-facing services, resource-facing services, provisioning workflows and network or OSS resources. Detailed subscriber provisioning records
remain in the customer boundary; product-level technical aggregates and governed metadata are sufficient for early rationalisation decisions.

Technical evidence security model
      Provisioning, subscriber and network-detail records stay within the Telco customer boundary.
      Customer-side queries calculate active instances, variants, workflows, exception rates and legacy dependencies by Product ID or Service
       Specification ID.
      Only approved metadata, aggregates and relationship keys enter the evidence snapshot.
      The UI shows evidence coverage separately for commercial equivalence, technical equivalence and execution readiness.
      A product can be commercially duplicate but technically different; the agent must recommend STANDARDIZE or MIGRATE before
       CONSOLIDATE.
      A commercially obsolete product with an active service footprint must be MIGRATE, not RETIRE.

14. Approval Completion and Control Rules
The scenario is complete when all mandatory approvers have acted, no blocking policy remains, and the decision state changes to APPROVED.
Approval does not directly change products. It enables the next controlled activity: generating a transition package.

    Control                                                                        Expected behaviour

    Evidence completeness                                                          Missing mandatory evidence forces INVESTIGATE and prevents unsafe submission.

    Decision versioning                                                            Every edit or override creates a new version; prior versions remain read-only.

    Approval routing                                                               Policy determines required roles based on disposition, revenue/customer impact and
                                                                                   regulatory conditions.

    Non-approval actions                                                           Reject, Return and Hold require a comment.

    Conflict control                                                               Self-approval can be disabled; approvals are limited to assigned role and scope.

    Audit                                                                          Actor, role, timestamp, authentication context, decision version, policy result and content
                                                                                   hash are retained.

    Next step                                                                      Only an APPROVED decision can generate a transition package; production execution
                                                                                   remains separately governed.



Quick success checklist
      Run scope, owner and objectives are complete.
      Source snapshot is approved and current.
      Candidate evidence covers customer, contract, revenue, service and system impact.
      Disposition, target, effective date and rationale are complete.
      Policy warnings have explicit treatment.
      All mandatory approvers act on the same decision version.

Scoring and assessment behavior
     Evidence completeness is calculated relative to the current candidate and emerging disposition.
     No missing aggregate is treated as a negative score. The affected dimension remains NOT ASSESSED.
     A preliminary recommendation may remain visible, but policy blocks the relevant approval when mandatory evidence is absent.
     Accepted evidence creates a new snapshot; scoring and assessment are recalculated with the pinned scoring and policy versions.
     If the returned evidence materially changes the decision, a new decision version and approval route are created.
